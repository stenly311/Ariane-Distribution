# Ariane Desktop – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 2, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Ariane Desktop ("Ariane", "the Application") is a standalone desktop application designed to help users manage, monitor, and analyze locally running processes and their performance.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information Ariane accesses, how it is processed and stored, when information may be exchanged with external services, and what control you have over your data.

Ariane follows a **local-first approach**, designed to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information.

## 2. Information Processed by Ariane

Ariane may access and process the following information to provide its functionality:

- Process information, including process names, process IDs (PIDs), executable paths, and runtime status.
- Performance measurements and monitoring history.
- Application configuration and user preferences.
- Application logs, diagnostic information, and error details.
- Information related to processes and services explicitly configured by the user.
- Connection settings required for external services configured by the user.

Depending on the processes being monitored, this information may contain personal, confidential, or commercially sensitive data.

Ariane processes this information primarily on the user's device to provide process management, monitoring, and diagnostic functionality.

## 3. Local Data Storage

Ariane is designed as a local-first application.

Application settings, monitoring history, measurements, and diagnostic logs are stored locally on the user's device.

By default, Ariane does not automatically transmit locally collected monitoring information to the developer's servers or external analytics platforms.

Users remain in control of their locally stored application data.

The exact storage locations and retention periods may vary depending on the application version, configuration, and operating system environment.

## 4. Network Connections and External Services

Ariane may establish network connections in the following circumstances:

**User-configured connections**

When users explicitly configure external messaging, monitoring, or integration services, Ariane may communicate with those services as required for the selected functionality.

The information exchanged depends on the configuration and the external service involved.

**Application updates**

Depending on the installation method, Ariane may communicate with update services to check for and retrieve application updates.

- **Microsoft Store installations:** Application updates are managed through Microsoft Store and its associated services.
- **Standalone installations:** Ariane may use its integrated update mechanism, including Velopack, to check for and download available updates from configured distribution endpoints.

These services may process technical information associated with network requests, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and application version information, according to their respective practices.

Ariane does not intentionally include locally collected process monitoring history in update requests.

**External service providers**

External services configured by users operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Ariane does not automatically upload locally collected monitoring history to the developer's servers.

## 5. Feedback, Bug Reports, and Technical Support

Users may voluntarily contact the developer to provide feedback, report bugs, request features, or obtain technical support.

Depending on the information voluntarily submitted, support requests may contain:

- Contact information, such as an email address or GitHub username.
- Application version and operating system details.
- Descriptions of errors or unexpected behavior.
- Diagnostic logs, screenshots, or other supporting information.

This information is used to respond to support requests, investigate reported issues, maintain application quality, and improve Ariane.

**Public GitHub Issues**

Ariane uses GitHub Issues as one of its support and feedback channels.

Information submitted through public GitHub Issues is publicly accessible and may be viewed, indexed, or retained by third parties.

Users should not include passwords, access tokens, connection strings, personal information, or confidential diagnostic data in public reports.

Users are encouraged to review and remove sensitive information from screenshots and diagnostic logs before sharing them.

**Security vulnerabilities**

Potential security vulnerabilities should be reported privately through the repository's GitHub security reporting functionality rather than through public Issues.

Where GDPR applies, the developer may process personal information voluntarily provided for support based on legitimate interests in responding to requests, maintaining security, and improving the Application, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Where consent is required for optional processing, it will be requested separately.

Support information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and applicable legal obligations. Public submissions may remain available according to GitHub's retention practices.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share locally stored monitoring information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed by third-party services in the following situations:

- When users explicitly configure external connections or integrations.
- When users submit information through GitHub or other support channels.
- When application installation or updates involve Microsoft Store or standalone update infrastructure.
- When service providers are required to process information for requested support or other legitimate operational purposes.

Microsoft and GitHub independently process certain information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.

The developer does not receive automatic access to locally stored monitoring data solely because the Application is installed or updated.

## 7. Data Security

Ariane relies on operating system security mechanisms and application-level safeguards to protect locally stored information.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security controls on their devices, including operating system updates, account permissions, and access to external services.

Because Ariane may interact with executable processes, local files, and user-configured connections, users should exercise care when configuring monitoring targets and sharing diagnostic information.

No software can guarantee absolute security. We aim to minimize unnecessary data collection, external transmission, and exposure of sensitive information.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, removed through application functionality, or affected by application uninstallation.

Some application files, including user-specific configuration, logs, or monitoring history, may remain after uninstallation and may require manual deletion.

Users may manage or remove locally stored information through available application functions or operating system tools.

Information voluntarily submitted through support channels is subject to the retention practices described in Section 5 and, where applicable, the policies of the relevant third-party service.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Where applicable under data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including:

- Access to personal information.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Deletion of personal information.
- Restriction of certain processing activities.
- Objection to processing based on legitimate interests.
- Data portability, where applicable.
- Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.
- Lodging a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

Because Ariane primarily processes information locally, the developer generally does not have access to data stored on users' devices.

Requests relating to personal information voluntarily submitted to the developer may be directed to the contact provided below.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Ariane is intended for developers, IT professionals, and other users managing desktop processes and services.

The Application is not specifically designed for children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its core local monitoring functionality.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality, data processing practices, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be made available through Ariane's official distribution or support channels.

Material changes affecting personal information processing will be communicated where required by applicable law.
